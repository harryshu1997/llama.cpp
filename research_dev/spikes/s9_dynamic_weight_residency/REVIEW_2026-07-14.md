# S9 R1 review - 2026-07-14

## Verdict

The reported DRAINING, stale-boot, allocation ownership, and served-island binding
repairs are real. v3 is still not dispatch authority. It validates static bundles
that disagree with authoritative device state, executable identity, transport
epochs, or physical state reservations.

## Repaired in this review

- A StateLease dependency must be one of the exact dispatched ResidencyLeases.
- A ReadyCertificate correctness certificate must belong to the dispatched island.
- Invalid v3 bundle fixtures bind exact expected error-code sets, and the bundle
  selftest rejects missing, phantom, or duplicate index entries.
- Shared-domain transfer release checks the exact phone/weight owner.
- A phone-scoped bulk fault cannot mutate another phone's transfer.
- Rejection executes deferred eviction at final unpin.
- One sticky/rebuildable session has at most one mutation in flight.
- Horizon timeouts release transient phone and server resources.
- Server GPU time is charged at admission, not before queue/rejection.
- Lane, ledger, state, pin, session, server-credit, and UFS ownership invariants run
  after every event.
- Simulator red evidence uses executable mutants where no historical snapshot exists.
- Golden tests compare the complete current manifest and pin frozen module hashes.
  V0 is correctly labeled self-consistency-only because its source config is absent.

## Remaining v3 blockers

Concrete mutation probes still validate when:

1. DeviceInventory is absent or has the wrong boot epoch.
2. DeviceInventory says accepting=false, draining=true, stale=true, or thermal
   ineligible.
3. A ResidencyLease is expired at dispatch time.
4. CorrectnessCertificate.backend_build differs from PreparedImage.backend_build.
5. Island model/version/graph identity differs from the prepared image or manifest.
6. PreparedImage.source_weight_set_id names a foreign set; that field is also absent
   from the v3 digest preimage.
7. EXECUTE or BULK transport epochs/command identity are stale or foreign.
8. TransferTicket issued boot/residency generation are absent from its digest preimage.
9. StateLease reserved state/activation bytes and mutation sequence are absent from
   its digest preimage.
10. StateLease reservations can exceed the device ledger.
11. DeviceInventory can declare a zero-live ledger while live allocations, images,
    leases, and state records exist.

Freeze v1-v3. Repair these under schema_version/bundle_version 4 with a domain-separated
v4 digest family and red-v3/green-v4 fixtures. Do not claim live dispatch certification:
atomic snapshot acquisition, compare-and-reserve use pins, and completion validation
remain V0c runtime work.
