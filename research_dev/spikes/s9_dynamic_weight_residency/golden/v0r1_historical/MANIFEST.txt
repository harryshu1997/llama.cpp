  The 11 fail-open cases the v3 suites did NOT catch (validator = wrongly ACCEPTS a static

    2  DeviceInventory says accepting=false / draining=true / stale=true / thermal ineligible.
    3  A ResidencyLease already expired (horizon.end_us) at the decision timestamp.
    4  CorrectnessCertificate.backend_build differs from PreparedImage.backend_build.
    5  Island model_id/model_version/graph_hash identity differs from the prepared image/manifest.
    6  PreparedImage.source_weight_set_id names a foreign set (also absent from the v3 digest).
    7  BULK transport epoch/device/command identity stale or foreign vs the ticket.
    8  EXECUTE transport epoch stack / request / device foreign vs the dispatch + state lease.
    9  TransferTicket issued_boot_epoch / issued_residency_generation absent from its v3 digest.
    10 StateLease reserved_state/activation bytes + in_flight_mutation_seq absent from its v3
       digest, and reservations able to exceed the device ledger.
    11 DeviceInventory declaring a zero-live (or otherwise non-derivable) ledger while live
       allocations / images / leases / state records exist.
