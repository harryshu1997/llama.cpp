How to read this

Coverage is the share of decode work moved off the host, not a share of requests. Gemma reaches 93 % because the phone holds all 24 of its feed-forward layers; Qwen reaches 43 % with 6 layers on the phone for part of the window.

The energy curves are integrated here from the 1 Hz RAPL and NVML samples the run recorded. The scheduler’s own accounting reports 203.3 kJ and 164.3 kJ for the two arms, within 0.2 % of the endpoints drawn above. Phone energy is not measured; it is assumed at 4.5 W active and booked separately at 2.8 kJ, which is under 2 % of the saving.

GPU energy is unchanged between the arms, 63.6 against 63.7 kJ: the whole saving is CPU package energy, 140.1 down to 101.0 kJ.
